Elle Minná is on a trip to visit her grandmother and grandfather with her reindeer, Muzetcuoivvat, but then a storm hits. How will she find her grandmother and grandfather? Luckily, she meets the fox who shows her the way to her grandmother and grandfather. During the drive, the sled sinks and a crash is heard. WHAT IS HAPPENING, Elle Minna thinks in horror?
