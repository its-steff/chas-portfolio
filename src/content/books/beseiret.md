Defeated is the sixth book in the epic children's and young adult book series about the ash elf Kha. The final book in the Dragon's Eye series offers a grand drama, with a battle between dragons and elves - all to win the battle for magic.

In the exciting ending, we will see the true faces of several of the main characters, and we promise that it will surprise readers.
